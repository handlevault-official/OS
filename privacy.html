Privacy Policy • HandleVault OS & Crisp Suite

Transparent Privacy & Strict No-Selling of User Data.

This Privacy Policy explains how HandleVault OS and Crisp Suite handle local browser drafts, session states, sign-in gates, cookies/advertising disclosures, and third-party storefront or support links across our static web platform.

Core Privacy Principles

No-Selling of User or Guest Data

Designed around static browser reliability and respectful handling of personal drafts, notes, and event details.

🔒 Zero Data Selling

We never sell, rent, or broker user or guest data to third parties. Guest list details, event RSVPs, personal notes, and creative drafts are not packaged for sale.

💾 Local Browser Storage

Draft notes, selected catalog stamps, theme preferences, and session flags stay in your browser's local storage so you can resume work without a central database.

🛡️ Clean Front-End Guardrails

We avoid storing passwords in custom databases, avoid exposing secret keys or private payment details in front-end code, and sanitize user-facing text.

Authentication & In-App Catalog

How Sign-In, Drafts & Stamp Catalogs Work

Clear visibility into what happens inside your browser when using the workspace and stamp catalog.

✦ Google Sign-In & Session State

Client-Side Access Gate

HandleVault OS uses Google Identity Services on the front gate and remembers your signed-in state in browser storage (crisp_logged_in) until you choose to sign out.

  • Sign out anytime to clear the local session flag in your browser
  • No raw authentication tokens or credentials are intentionally logged to public consoles
  • Theme preferences (hv_theme) and drafts (hv_letter_draft) remain local
✦ Native Emoji Catalog & Preview

No System Keyboard Modification

Curated stamp packs—whether used for 3D letters, journals, study notes, or everyday entries—work through in-app catalog selection and live preview using native Unicode emojis.

  • Stamps are not automatically installed into your phone or computer system keyboard
  • Main stamp packs are priced at $1 USD / ₱29 Local with optional add-ons/custom tiers
  • External purchases occur on third-party storefronts such as Gumroad

✦ Advertising & Cookies

Cookie & AdSense Disclosure

Where Google AdSense or ad placements are active on free tiers, third-party vendors (including Google) may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Upgrading to Pro Tier removes AdSense placements, sponsor bars, and preview watermarks
  • You can manage cookies or ad preferences directly through your browser settings
✦ Storefronts & Community

Third-Party Checkouts & Support

When you click external links—such as Gumroad for stamp packs, Ko-fi for creator support, or external community forums—you interact with those services under their respective privacy policies.

  • Reserved payment slots (such as PayMongo or other gateways) do not embed raw account numbers or secret keys in public code
  • External community forums keep discussions separate from the static OS core
